# Week 1-2: Foundations of Physical AI

## Topics
- **Overview of the Humanoid Landscape**: From Asimo to Optimus.
- **Sensor Systems**:
  - **LIDAR**: 2D vs 3D mapping.
  - **IMUs**: The "Inner Ear" for balance (Accelerometers/Gyroscopes).
  - **Depth Cameras**: RealSense RGB-D for perception.
  - **Force/Torque Sensors**: Knowing when you are touching the world.

## Learning Goals
- Identify key sensor types required for SLAM.
- Understand the coordinate frames in robotics (TF2).
