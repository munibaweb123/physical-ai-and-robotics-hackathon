# From Digital Brains to Embodied Intelligence

**Physical AI** represents the transition of artificial intelligence from purely digital environments (chatbots, image generators) to the physical world (robots, actuators).

## The Core Shift

Traditional AI operates in a world of infinite data and zero consequences. Physical AI must operate under:
1. **Gravity & Inertia**: Physics cannot be ignored.
2. **Noise**: Real sensors are imperfect.
3. **Time**: Computation must happen in real-time (milliseconds matter).

## Embodied Intelligence

Embodied intelligence posits that intelligence emerges from the interaction between an agent and its environment. A "Brain in a Jar" cannot truly understand "heavy" or "slippery" without sensorimotor experience.

In this course, we build systems that:
- **Perceive** the world (Sensors).
- **Plan** actions (LLMs/Planners).
- **Act** upon the world (Actuators).
