# Background Data Privacy

## What Information We Collect

When you sign up for our service, you have the option to provide information about your software and hardware background to help us personalize your experience. This includes:

- **Software Experience Level**: Your level of experience with software development (beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert)
- **Hardware Experience Level**: Your knowledge and experience with hardware (beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert)
- **Preferred Development Environments**: The development tools and environments you prefer to use (e.g., VSCode, IntelliJ, Vim)
- **Technical Skills**: Programming languages, frameworks, and technologies you're familiar with
- **Hardware Specifications**: Information about your current hardware setup

## How We Use Your Information

The background information you provide is used solely to:

- Personalize content recommendations based on your experience level
- Suggest relevant tutorials, resources, and documentation
- Tailor the user experience to your technical preferences
- Improve the relevance of search results and content discovery

## Data Storage and Security

- Your background information is stored securely in our database
- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data
- Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest
- We do not share your background information with third parties

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access**: View all background information we store about you
- **Update**: Modify your background information at any time through your profile settings
- **Delete**: Remove your background information completely (this will affect personalization)

To exercise any of these rights, visit your profile settings or contact our support team.

## Data Retention

We retain your background information as long as your account is active. If you choose to delete your account, your background information will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.
