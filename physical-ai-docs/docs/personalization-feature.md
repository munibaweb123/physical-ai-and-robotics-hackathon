# Content Personalization Feature

## Overview

Our platform offers content personalization to enhance your learning experience. When you're logged in, you can personalize the content in chapters to match your background, experience level, and preferences.

## How It Works

1. **Profile Collection**: During signup, we ask about your software and hardware experience, technical skills, and development preferences.
2. **Chapter Personalization**: At the start of each chapter, you'll see a "Personalize this content" button.
3. **Content Adaptation**: When activated, the system adapts the content based on your profile information:
   - Adjusts complexity level based on your experience
   - Substitutes examples with technologies you're familiar with
   - Emphasizes topics relevant to your interests

## Privacy Notice

The background information you provide is used solely to personalize your experience and recommend relevant content. We do not share this information with third parties. You can update or remove this information at any time from your profile settings.

## Managing Your Personalization Settings

You can manage your personalization preferences by:
- Updating your profile information in the profile settings page
- Toggling personalization on/off for individual chapters
- Adjusting specific preferences like complexity level or focus areas

## Technical Details

The personalization engine analyzes your profile information including:
- Software and hardware experience level
- Technical skills and preferred technologies
- Development environment preferences
- Hardware specifications

Based on this information, the system adapts content in real-time to provide an optimal learning experience matched to your needs.
