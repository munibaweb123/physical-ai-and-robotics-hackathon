# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Course Overview

Welcome to the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics course! This curriculum is designed to guide you through the exciting convergence of digital intelligence and physical embodiment. We will explore the fundamental concepts, advanced platforms, and practical applications of building intelligent humanoid robots.

## Course Structure

This course is divided into four main parts, plus a set of appendices:

- **Part I: Foundations of Embodied Intelligence**: Dive into the core principles that govern physical AI systems.
- **Part II: The Robotic Nervous System**: Master the tools and frameworks for robot communication and simulation, focusing on ROS 2.
- **Part III: The AI-Robot Brain**: Explore advanced control systems and AI platforms like NVIDIA Isaac™ for sophisticated robot behaviors.
- **Part IV: The Cognitive Convergence**: Integrate cutting-edge AI models for vision, language, and action to create truly autonomous humanoids.
- **Appendices**: Supplementary materials including lab setup guides, hardware bills of materials, and troubleshooting tips.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

- Understand the unique challenges and advantages of embodied intelligence.
- Set up and configure a physical AI development environment.
- Develop and simulate robotic systems using ROS 2 and digital twins.
- Implement advanced control algorithms for humanoid locomotion and manipulation.
- Integrate large language models and vision systems for cognitive robotics.
- Design and execute a capstone project for an autonomous humanoid.

Let's begin our journey into the future of robotics!
